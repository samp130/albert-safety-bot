<h1 align="center">🛡️ Albert — Your Safe Space</h1>

<p align="center">
  <b>A safe, anonymous support system for Indian students (ages 10–16) facing cyberbullying or online fraud.</b><br/>
  Available on Telegram. Free. Confidential. No sign-up required.
</p>

---

## 💙 What Is Albert?

Albert is a friendly AI on Telegram that helps students who are being bullied online, harassed, or scammed. You can talk to Albert in complete safety — **no one will ever know it was you**.

If what you're going through is serious, Albert will quietly connect you with a real trained volunteer who can help — without ever revealing your name or your Telegram account.

---

## 🚀 How to Use Albert

1. **Open Telegram** on your phone or computer.
2. Search for your school/organization's Albert bot (your teacher or school admin will share the exact link).
3. Tap **Start** and choose what you need:
   - **🆘 I Need Help** — if something bad happened to you online
   - **👩‍🏫 I'm a Teacher / Adult Helper** — if you're a teacher or parent trying to support a student

That's it. Albert will guide you from there.

---

## 🔒 Is It Safe? Will Anyone Know?

**Yes, it's completely safe. Here's what Albert promises:**

- ✅ **No names, no accounts** — Albert never asks for your name, school name, phone number, or any personal details.
- ✅ **Anonymous relay** — If you speak to a volunteer counselor through Albert, neither of you can see the other's identity. Messages pass through the bot like a sealed envelope.
- ✅ **Panic Wipe** — At any point, tap the **🚨 Panic Wipe** button. Albert instantly replaces the entire chat with fake NCERT Math content. If someone grabs your phone, they'll see nothing but homework. All your data is permanently deleted.
- ✅ **No storage** — Albert keeps nothing. The moment you close the session, it's gone. Nothing is saved to any server or database.

---

## 📞 What Happens When I Report Something?

Albert asks you **3 short questions** to understand what happened. Based on your answers:

| Situation | What Albert Does |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Minor incident, you're mostly okay | Gives you helpful tips and resources |
| Ongoing harassment, you're upset | Gives resources and offers to connect you with a trained volunteer |
| Serious threat, danger, or blackmail | Immediately connects you with a volunteer counselor |
| Anything involving sexual abuse or exploitation | **Immediately** gives you **Childline 1098** — this is not handled by volunteers |

---

## 👩‍🏫 For Teachers & Adults

If one of your students came to you about something online, tap **"I'm a Teacher / Adult Helper"** when you open Albert. Describe what the student told you, and Albert will give you:

- Practical steps to help the student right now
- How to respond with empathy (without accidentally making it worse)
- When and how to escalate to authorities

## 🆘 Emergency Contacts

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, please contact:

| Service | Number | Available |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| **Childline India** | **1098** | 24/7, Free, Confidential |
| **Cyber Crime Helpline** | **1930** | 24/7 |
| **Cyber Crime Portal** | [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in) | Online reporting |
| **Police Emergency** | **100** | 24/7 |

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
No. Just open Telegram and start chatting with the bot.

**Q: Will my parents or teachers find out?**
No. Albert does not contact anyone without your knowledge, and all data is wiped when the session ends.

**Q: What if I make a mistake while typing?**
Don't worry. Albert is designed to be understanding, especially if you're nervous. There are no wrong answers.

**Q: What if I just need to talk to someone right now?**
Call **Childline 1098** — it's free, available 24/7, and completely confidential.
